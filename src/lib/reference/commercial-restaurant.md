# Inspection reference — Commercial restaurants (France)

This reference condenses the applicable texts and the inspection grid used by the French State
services (DDPP / DDETSPP, DGAL "restauration commerciale" vade-mecum). Every finding must be
tied to a reference below.

## 1. Applicable texts

| Ref. | Text | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| EC 852/2004 | Regulation (EC) No 852/2004, art. 4-5 and Annex II | General food hygiene, HACCP obligation |
| EC 178/2002 | Regulation (EC) No 178/2002, art. 14, 18, 19 | Food safety, traceability, withdrawal/recall |
| EU 1169/2011 | Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011 + décret n° 2015-447 | Information on the 14 mandatory allergens |
| AM 21/12/2009 | Arrêté du 21 décembre 2009 (products of animal origin) | Storage temperatures, hot/cold chain, freezing |
| AM 08/10/2013 | Arrêté du 8 octobre 2013 (other foodstuffs) | Temperatures and rules for non-animal foodstuffs |
| AM 05/10/2011 | Arrêté du 5 octobre 2011 + décret n° 2011-731 | Mandatory 14-hour hygiene training in commercial catering |
| AM 15/12/2016 | Arrêté du 15 décembre 2016 | Alim'confiance: publication of inspection results |
| D 2008-184 | Décret n° 2008-184 | Frying oils: polar compounds ≤ 25 % |
| CSP L.1321 | Code de la santé publique, art. L.1321-1 ff. | Water intended for human consumption |

## 2. Regulatory temperatures (AM 21/12/2009, Annexes I and II)

| Foodstuff / situation | Limit |
|---|---|
| Minced meat, minced-meat preparations | ≤ +2 °C |
| Offal, meat preparations, poultry, rabbit | ≤ +4 °C |
| Fresh fish and fishery products | melting ice, 0 to +2 °C |
| Other perishable foods (dairy, eggs, sliced deli, ready-to-use vegetables) | ≤ +4 °C |
| Pre-cooked dishes, cold chain (cook-chill) | ≤ +3 °C |
| Hot chain, hot holding, direct hot service | ≥ +63 °C |
| Frozen products | ≤ −12 °C |
| Deep-frozen products, ice cream | ≤ −18 °C |
| Rapid cooling | from +63 °C to +10 °C in less than 2 h |
| Reheating | from +10 °C to +63 °C in less than 1 h |
| Thawing | in a refrigerated unit ≤ +4 °C, never at room temperature |
| Thawed product | consumed within 3 days, never refrozen |
| Cooked-then-chilled dish made on site | consumed by D+3 at ≤ +3 °C |

Tolerance: an isolated +2 °C excursion on a chilled unit during a door opening is acceptable if
documented and corrected; a larger or persistent excursion (several consecutive readings)
constitutes a cold-chain failure.

## 3. Inspection grid — chapters and control points

Each point is rated A (compliant), B (minor non-compliance), C (medium non-compliance),
D (major non-compliance). Points marked **[critical]** can alone lead to an overall grade of
"urgent correction".

### A. Premises and equipment (EC 852/2004, Annex II, ch. I, II, V)
- A1. Layout: forward flow in space or in time, separation of dirty/clean sectors.
- A2. Floors, walls, ceilings, doors: smooth, washable, in good repair (no broken tiles, flaking paint, mould).
- A3. Hand-wash sink: present in the kitchen, hands-free operation, hot water, liquid soap, single-use towels, separate from the pot-wash sink.
- A4. Ventilation and hood: working, clean filters, no condensation.
- A5. Adequate lighting, shatter-protected.
- A6. Cold equipment: sufficient capacity, thermometer per unit, clean and tight gaskets, no excessive frost, nothing stored on the floor.
- A7. Work surfaces, utensils, boards: food-contact-safe materials, not deteriorated (raw wood boards, rusty utensils = non-compliance).
- A8. Drains, floor traps: clean, no stagnation, no odour.
- A9. Windows and openings: insect screens or closed during production.
- A10. Staff changing rooms and toilets: separate from the kitchen, not opening directly onto handling areas.

### B. Operation and practices (EC 852/2004, Annex II, ch. IX; AM 21/12/2009)
- B1. Receiving: temperature and use-by checks on delivery, documented refusals.
- B2. **[critical]** Storage temperatures compliant with table § 2, recorded and logged.
- B3. Storage: nothing on the floor (≥ 15 cm or on racks), raw and cooked separated, products protected (wrapped or in closed containers), no cardboard inside cold units.
- B4. Secondary labelling: opening / production date and secondary use-by date on every opened or house-made product.
- B5. **[critical]** Expired use-by dates in stock or spoiled products (smell, appearance, mould).
- B6. **[critical]** Cross-contamination: raw above cooked, same board for raw meat and vegetables, shell eggs in contact with finished products, allergens not isolated.
- B7. Cooling and reheating controlled and logged (blast chiller or equivalent method).
- B8. Thawing in a cold unit, thawed products identified.
- B9. On-site freezing: only with suitable equipment, freezing-date labels, written procedure.
- B10. Frying oils: polar-compound test or documented change frequency.
- B11. Potable water: public network or analyses; ice made from potable water.
- B12. Waste: hands-free, closed bins, emptied regularly, isolated and clean waste area, used oils collected.
- B13. **[critical]** Pests: no trace (droppings, rodents, cockroaches, flies in numbers); control plan, mapped traps/baits, contractor agreement.
- B14. Cleaning and disinfection: written plan (who, what, when, how, products), food-safe products stored away from food, technical sheets available.
- B15. Traceability (EC 178/2002 art. 18): meat and sensitive-product labels kept, delivery notes archived, products traceable to the supplier.
- B16. Allergens (EU 1169/2011): written information available to consumers, recipe sheets up to date.

### C. Food safety management plan (PMS) — documents (EC 852/2004, art. 5)
- C1. Written good hygiene practices (staff, premises, cleaning, pests, water, temperatures, traceability).
- C2. Hazard analysis and CCPs: process flow diagrams, hazards identified, critical limits, monitoring, corrective actions.
- C3. Records kept: temperature logs (at least twice a day), receiving, cleaning, oils, cooling, non-compliances.
- C4. Non-compliance and withdrawal/recall procedure.
- C5. Hygiene training certificate (AM 05/10/2011) for at least one person in the establishment.
- C6. Activity declaration (Cerfa 13984) filed with the DDPP.

### D. Staff (EC 852/2004, Annex II, ch. VIII)
- D1. Clean, dedicated work attire changed daily; dedicated shoes; hair cover.
- D2. Hand washing: practice observed, procedure displayed.
- D3. Hygiene: short unvarnished nails, no jewellery, wounds covered, no smoking/drinking/eating in production areas.
- D4. Medical follow-up and exclusion of sick staff (gastroenteritis, throat infection, infected skin lesions).
- D5. Ongoing training and instructions displayed.

## 4. Severity scale and Alim'confiance grade (AM 15/12/2016)

Severity of a non-compliance:
- **minor**: upkeep or documentation defect with no direct consumer risk (incomplete secondary labelling, dirty gasket, missing signage).
- **major**: loss of control that may lead to a hazard (temperature not logged, no cleaning plan, occasional raw/cooked misplacement, unusable hand-wash sink).
- **critical**: proven or imminent consumer hazard (confirmed cold-chain failure, expired products in stock, active pests, widespread cross-contamination, no potable water, insalubrity).

Mandatory qualification of temperature findings (B2), one per unit, applied as is:
- unit with at least one reading flagged "PERSISTENT DRIFT" (≥ 2 consecutive readings out of range) → **critical**;
- isolated excursion beyond tolerance (> 2 °C on chilled units, any excursion on frozen or hot holding) → **major**;
- isolated excursion within the 2 °C door-opening tolerance on a chilled unit → **minor**.

Overall grade prediction rules:
1. ≥ 1 uncontrolled **critical** non-compliance → **urgent_correction** (closure risk: high; formal notice or immediate administrative closure possible).
2. ≥ 3 **major** non-compliances, or 1 major combined with ≥ 5 minors → **to_improve** (risk: moderate; warning, formal notice, re-inspection).
3. Only minors, or 1-2 isolated majors with an existing PMS → **satisfactory** (risk: low).
4. No non-compliance, or only improvement remarks → **very_satisfactory**.

A missing or empty PMS (chapter C) cannot lead to "very_satisfactory" and is a major
non-compliance in itself.

## 5. Deadlines expected by the inspector
- **immediate**: removal of expired or out-of-temperature food, isolation of an infested area, stopping a cross-contamination practice.
- **24h**: repair of a cold unit, replacement of deteriorated equipment, cleaning of an insalubrious area.
- **7d**: setting up records, written cleaning plan, signage, pest treatment by a contractor.
- **30d**: works on the premises, full PMS drafting, staff training.
